# Company ownership: ready for independent review

Implementation is finished in the feature worktree. No commit, push, PR, merge, deployment or publication has been performed. The earlier independent-ledger checkpoint has been superseded by simulation and UI integration.

Every company starts with a named private founder owning 1,000 shares. Founders have persistent private investor accounts, distinct from physical city household needs and property ownership; each starts with $1,500. This also gives companies in worlds without households real private owners. Industry additions receive the same ownership initialization. Only a company's recorded founder can authorize its initial public offering. The mayor's Exchange screen directs decisions for the selected simulated owner, investor or company account, consistent with the existing shared city management tools. A network player's entity number is not used as a shareholder identity.

The city can place one stock exchange special building using the isometric Exchange tool, at a clear site beside a road, for $600. It is a walkable office with desks and lighting. Placement checks budget, city boundaries, roads, existing buildings, projects and player occupancy before applying edits or charging. It assigns three university graduate office roles and one support role. The founding settlement includes four graduates; legacy worlds seed the same founding positions. Qualifications persist separately from income cohorts. Staff commute through normal city travel and receive treasury-funded hourly wages. Trading requires at least two paid graduates on site, a graduate majority, and office hours of 08:00–17:00. Closed or unfunded exchanges preserve orders and permit their owners to cancel.

The Exchange dashboard shows company status, founder, issued shares, ownership percentages, shares held/free, available account cash, last trade price, orders and recent trades. Select a company and actor, enter an integer share quantity and exact dollar limit price, then choose Go public, Buy, Sell or Cancel selected. Review and confirm explicitly. IPO sales fund the company. Secondary sales pay the seller. Cash and share reservations prevent overspending and short selling. Crossing limit orders use price/time priority and the resting price, with partial fills and cash refunds for price improvement. No random or prescribed price movement is introduced.

Save format CITY7 and network protocol 15 carry investors, qualifications, listings, holdings, escrowed orders and bounded recent trades. CITY1–6 remain readable; old companies upgrade to private ownership without changing historical company cash, buildings, clock, materials or contracts. Restoration never charges buy escrow again. Invalid owners, duplicate records, invalid prices/quantities and share conservation failures are rejected. Exchange availability is derived from live office conditions.

Verification: `company-ownership-tests.json` records the successful commands and 181 sandbox-compatible regression tests, with zero failures/errors/skips. The final focused run passes all 22 ownership, settlement, integration and UI tests, including natural commuting, treasury wages, malformed snapshots, save/network round trips and explicit confirmation. Three socket-dependent suites could not execute in this sandbox and need CI verification; the original full run reported `Operation not permitted` on server socket creation.
