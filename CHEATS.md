# Offline cheat mode

Press **F4** to enter or leave cheat mode. Entry enables flight. Use Space to rise,
Ctrl to descend, WASD to move, and Shift to move faster. F still toggles flight.
Exit disables flight. Leave your vehicle before entering or leaving the mode.

While cheat mode is on:

- **Page Up** selects the next vehicle. The list wraps: Jeep, Container truck,
  Liquid tanker, Delivery van, Goods lorry, Passenger jet, Cargo carrier.
- **Page Down** spawns the selected vehicle ahead of you. Stand near loaded ground
  in a clear area. Spawns reject blocks, parked vehicles, jets and unloaded space.
- **Insert** adds **$10,000** to the city treasury in Voxel City One. Grants stop
  when the budget exceeds $990,000,000.

The road vehicles use normal driving controls. Walk near the cab and press J to
enter or exit. Existing parked vehicles remain available during the session.
Passenger jets and cargo carriers are static assets, as in normal city play;
spawning does not create a flight booking, airport, port or transport route.
There is a limit of 64 successful spawns per session.

Cheats are available offline. Each action checks the session. Controls can be
changed in the controls menu. F6, F7, F8, F9 and F10 keep their existing functions.

Treasury grants, carrier blocks and the current road vehicle use existing saves.
Extra parked road vehicles and spawned jets last for the session. Cheat mode and
its selected vehicle reset when the game starts.
