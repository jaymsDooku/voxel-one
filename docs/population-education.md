# Citizens and education

Citizens have a saved age, gender, spouse, parents and education. A year takes 12 city days. Day length changes real time, but does not change the number of city days in a year.

Adults older than 18 can marry. Parents, children and siblings cannot marry. A married couple can share a home if they can pay for a free place. Couples with a shared home and a free place can have children. Births are spaced at least two years apart. Mothers can have children until age 45. The city supports up to 128 citizens. Children share their mother's home and receive money for food from her. Citizens under 18 cannot work.

Use **Special** to place primary schools, secondary schools, universities or technical colleges beside a road. Each has three permit levels, with 8, 16 or 24 student places. Citizens choose the nearest school with a free place. At ages 18 to 21, this choice determines their university or technical path.

| School | Ages | Required prior education | Attendance to graduate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | 5–11 | None | 6 school years |
| Secondary | 12–17 | Primary | 5 school years |
| University | 18–21 | Secondary | 3 school years |
| Technical college | 18–21 | Secondary | 3 school years |

Classes run from 08:00 to 14:00. Only time physically inside the school counts. A school year is 12 days of six hours in class. Missing a road route, a place, the age range or the required prior education prevents progress. Partial study resets when a citizen leaves the age range; completed qualifications remain. Students do not work during class hours. Travel speed follows the chosen day length, so shorter days do not leave pupils spending their whole class time on the road. School routes end in the clear central aisle. Students use the same meal threshold as workers during class hours.

Citizens with no education or only primary education can do manual work such as mining, logging, excavation, construction and farming. Secondary education opens commercial jobs. Technical education opens factory jobs. University education opens office jobs. Higher qualifications retain commercial and manual opportunities; university does not replace technical training. The stock exchange has four university-qualified office roles. Adult graduates review their jobs immediately and can move from a protected commercial post into a newly qualified factory or office vacancy.

Inspect a citizen to see age, gender, family, school and study progress. Inspect a school to see ages, prerequisites, attendance requirements and occupied places. Existing saves migrate to adult citizens and starting qualifications. New saves use CITY9; multiplayer uses protocol 19. School, family and study data survive saving and reconnecting.
