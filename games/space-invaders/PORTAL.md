# Cinder Quay campaign chapter

The explicit campaign is classic → fighter → descent → alien-ground → alternate-earth-ground. Defeat the twelve pilots in level 3. The last kill scores once and clears projectiles and uncollected pickups. A device forms a portal on a nearby clear, reachable patch. Opening freezes simulation for preparation; once ready, movement, looking and firing remain available. Walk through the opening. Touch the frame or fire at it without transferring. If preparation fails, Retry or Cancel preserves the completed source. After cancel, E / INTERACT activates the device again.

The threshold shows a bounded original street preview. A short restrained light fade precedes arrival; no camera warp or shake is used, including with reduced motion. Source and destination yaw frames preserve facing. Transfer preserves score, lives, health, blaster, loaded ammunition and the same run ID. Reload and attack timers, input and source projectiles are cleared. The district gets its own mission/world identity and seed. Simulation time is not set to the story year. Take control / Resume is required on arrival; pointer lock is requested only from that click. A failed or denied lock supports drag LOOK, arrows and F / FIRE.

Cinder Quay is a fictional American industrial harbour under Nazi occupation in alternate 1964 after an Axis victory. Occupation guards are antagonists. Mara Vale, an original resistance printer, offers the first objective. Go west to Vale Print and use E / INTERACT near the counter. Interact again to read the dated underground newspaper. Cross the occupation checkpoint by the tram street, warehouse lane or quay routes, then disable the north-quay relay with E / INTERACT. Guards use the shared ground navigation, cover, opaque rays and combat rules, with configured yellow projectiles. Civilians and the contact have separate silhouettes, solid bodies and dialogue; they are not hostile targets and never award kills or score. Guard elimination does not replace the relay objective. Completion ends the chapter; Restart campaign returns to level 1.

Death respawns at the current district checkpoint without replaying level 3. Brief arrival/checkpoint immunity is labelled. Escape, hidden tab, blur, rotation and context loss pause and reset controls. Loading and the threshold fade freeze with pause. Settings carry in the same session. The campaign remains session-only: reload does not restore a run, and no save format is claimed.

`PortalCoordinator` alone owns locked → opening → ready → traversing → consumed, plus recoverable failure. Preparation has an eight-second active-simulation deadline and an epoch fence against late replies. Source CPU state remains until commit. One destination CPU world is prepared; no second GPU renderer or live portal camera is allocated. The street preview uses a fixed number of boxes. Shared ground mesh limits still apply (16 MiB); fine chunks remain one job per frame. Destination has six guards and three noncombatants, bounded props, at most 32 projectiles, 12 trails and 16 pickups. Those limits bound allocations, not physical-device frame rate.

For repeat tests, localhost only accepts `?devScene=portal` (fresh level 3) and `?devScene=earth` (fresh district). These fixtures do not mark pilots defeated or objectives complete. Hosted normal campaigns have no query shortcut. Browser harness fixtures and limitations are recorded in the report. Browser emulation does not establish physical iPhone or GPU performance.
